History & Leadership

Founded in 1994, built on relationships, integrity, and performance.

From humble beginnings in Bugurini Malapa in 1994, founders Tania and Hamish Hamilton set out to redefine construction material supply in Tanzania. Over three decades, Nabaki Afrika has evolved under the visionary leadership of CEO Russel Stuart into an award-winning industry powerhouse with multiple nationwide branches, over 120 dedicated team members, and a legacy of quality.
